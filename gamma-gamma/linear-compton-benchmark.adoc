== Linear Compton Benchmark

=== Scope

We consider a single ultra-relativistic electron with total energy stem:[E_e]
and rest mass stem:[m_e]. The electron moves along stem:[+\hat z].

The laser is represented by a single photon with wavelength stem:[\lambda_L]
and propagates along stem:[+\hat x]. This corresponds to a crossing angle of
stem:[90^\circ] between the beam and the laser.

The benchmark quantity is the energy of the scattered photon emitted in the
forward electron direction stem:[+\hat z].

=== Units

The present benchmark follows the conventions already used in the OPALX source:

- electron and photon energies are expressed in `GeV`
- wavelength is expressed in `m`
- the speed of light stem:[c] and the reduced Planck constant stem:[\hbar] are
  taken from `Physics.h`

=== Laser Photon Energy

For a laser wavelength stem:[\lambda_L], the photon energy is

[stem]
++++
\omega_L = \frac{2 \pi \hbar c}{\lambda_L}.
++++

This is the same expression used in the current `Laser` helper in OPALX.

=== Exact 90 Degree Kinematics

The incoming electron momentum magnitude is

[stem]
++++
p_e = \sqrt{E_e^2 - m_e^2}.
++++

For the 90 degree crossing geometry, the linear Compton invariant used by the
first OPALX helper reduces to

[stem]
++++
x = \frac{2 E_e \omega_L}{m_e^2}.
++++

For a photon emitted in the forward electron direction, exact energy-momentum
conservation gives

[stem]
++++
\omega_\gamma' =
\frac{E_e \, \omega_L}
     {E_e - p_e + \omega_L}.
++++

For relativistic electrons the term stem:[E_e - p_e] is numerically small, so
the equivalent but more stable form is preferred in code and tests:

[stem]
++++
\omega_\gamma' =
\frac{E_e \, \omega_L}
     {\omega_L + \frac{m_e^2}{E_e + p_e}}.
++++

=== Relation to CAIN

CAIN evaluates the linear Compton kinematics by boosting the laser photon into
the electron rest frame, applying the Compton recoil there, and boosting the
scattered photon back to the laboratory frame.

In the CAIN source this appears in the linear Compton generator `LNCPGN`, where
the incoming laser photon energy in the electron rest frame is

[stem]
++++
\omega_1 = \gamma \, \omega_L \, (1 - \beta \cos \alpha),
++++

with crossing-angle cosine stem:[\cos \alpha].

For the present benchmark stem:[\cos \alpha = 0], so

[stem]
++++
\omega_1 = \gamma \, \omega_L.
++++

The OPALX helper currently reproduces this linear, fixed-geometry limit and the
unit test checks it against the independent exact laboratory-frame formula
above.

=== Current OPALX Status

The current implementation is intentionally narrow:

- the `LASER` element stores the laser wavelength and direction
- a helper computes the laser photon energy and the simplest linear Compton
  benchmark quantities
- a unit test verifies the exact 90 degree forward-scattering result

What is not included yet:

- finite pulse overlap
- luminosity integration
- photon bunch population
- differential spectra beyond the single forward benchmark
- nonlinear Compton effects
- Breit-Wheeler pair creation

=== Next Steps

The next natural benchmark is a differential linear Compton spectrum for a
fixed geometry, either

- against an analytic kernel in the unpolarized limit, or
- against a CAIN reference case with fixed beam energy, laser wavelength, and
  crossing angle.
